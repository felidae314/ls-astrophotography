About Me

Hi, I'm Levi, an amateur astrophotographer from the Netherlands. Through LS Astrophotography I enjoy exploring and capturing the beauty of the night sky, from our own Solar System to distant galaxies and nebulae millions of light-years away.

I like to experiment with tracking, guiding, processing workflows and long-term projects, documenting both the technical side and the visual results in this portfolio.
